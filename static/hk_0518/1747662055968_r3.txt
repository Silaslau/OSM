1. **Kwun Hoi Terrace (Left 70 to Left 10 Degree):**
   Located to the left side of our viewpoint, spanning from 70 degrees to 10 degrees, you can see the impressive Kwun Hoi Terrace. These are tall buildings characterized by numerous windows and balconies, giving them a distinctive and bustling appearance. The buildings are situated approximately 20 to 50 meters away from us. Adjoining the terrace, you will notice a highway service and a private access road, which add to the dynamic nature of this area.

2. **Kwun Hoi Terrace (Left 10 to Right 10 Degree):**
   Moving slightly to the center of our viewpoint, from 10 degrees left to 10 degrees right, the Kwun Hoi Terrace continues to dominate the scene. These buildings maintain their tall stature with many windows and balconies, similar to the previous section. However, this part of the terrace is a bit further away, approximately 50 to 100 meters from our current position. Like the previous section, it is also accompanied by a highway service and a private access road, seamlessly integrating urban living with essential services.
