1. **Wah Tong Building:**
   Located between the left 70 to left 30 degree angle, the Wah Tong Building is a tall structure characterized by numerous windows and air conditioning units. It stands approximately 30 meters from our current position. This building is notable for housing Lee's Optical Company and Home Curtain Decoration. Additionally, it is flanked by other buildings, creating a dense urban environment.

2. **Chit Lee Commercial Building:**
   Positioned between the left 30 to left 10 degree angle, the Chit Lee Commercial Building is another tall edifice with many windows and air conditioning units, similar to the Wah Tong Building. It is also about 30 meters away from us. This building includes notable tenants such as Dr. Keith Lee and the Suzuki Piano Centre, and is surrounded by other buildings, contributing to the bustling commercial atmosphere.

3. **Traffic Accident and Shau Kei Wan Reporting Centre:**
   Situated from the center to the right 10 degree angle, this building is approximately 50 meters away. It features various shops and serves as a reporting center for traffic accidents, indicating its importance in public safety and community services.
