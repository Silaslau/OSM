1. Tai Po Tsai:
Located within the left 70 to right 10 degree range of our view, Tai Po Tsai is a cluster of multi-story residential buildings. These buildings are characterized by their beige and white exteriors, which give them a clean and modern appearance. The red-tiled roofs add a touch of traditional charm, creating a visually appealing contrast with the lighter walls. The buildings are situated approximately 5 to 30 meters from our current position, making them a prominent feature in the scene.
