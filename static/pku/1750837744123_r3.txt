1. Lui Che Woo Building:
Located from the left 70 to right 10 degree angle of our viewpoint, the Lui Che Woo Building is a prominent multi-story structure. Its facade proudly displays the name 'LUI CHE WOO BUILDING,' making it easily identifiable. The building is approximately 10 to 20 meters away from us, giving us a clear view of its impressive height and modern design. Adjoining the building, you can see a highway footway, which adds to the urban landscape. Additionally, within the building, there is a Luckin Coffee, a popular spot for grabbing a quick coffee or snack.
