1. Philosophy Building:
Located within the left 70 to right 10 degree range of our viewpoint, the Philosophy Building is a striking structure that immediately catches the eye. Its facade is composed of gray bricks, giving it a solid and timeless appearance. The red window frames provide a vibrant contrast to the gray bricks, adding a touch of color and character to the building. This building is approximately 10 to 30 meters away from us, making it quite prominent in the scene. Surrounding the Philosophy Building, you can see a highway footway, which adds to the accessibility and connectivity of the area.
