1. **Boya Tower:**
   Located within the left 10 to right 10 degree range of our view, the Boya Tower is a striking multi-story traditional pagoda structure. Its intricate design features multiple tiers, each adorned with gracefully upturned eaves, typical of classical East Asian architecture. The tower's exterior is a harmonious blend of rich reds and earthy browns, with ornate detailing that captures the eye. Standing approximately 20 meters away from us, the Boya Tower is a prominent and captivating feature in the scene, embodying both cultural heritage and architectural elegance.
