1. **New Sun Student Center:**
   Located within the left 30 to right 30 degree range of our viewpoint, the New Sun Student Center is a prominent feature in the scene. This large, multi-story building is easily recognizable by its gray facade and numerous windows, which give it a modern and welcoming appearance. The building is approximately 30 meters away from us, making it a central and accessible hub for students and visitors alike. Adjoining the Student Center, you will find several key features including access permissive pathways, multiple highway footways, highway steps, and a bridge, all of which contribute to the ease of movement around the area. Additionally, the inclusive node 青年研究中心 (Youth Research Center) is associated with this location, indicating a space dedicated to youth-oriented research and activities.
