1. 34A and 34B Buildings:
Located within the left 70 to right 10-degree range of our viewpoint, the 34A and 34B Buildings are prominent features in this scene. These buildings are characterized by their striking white and blue exteriors, which create a visually appealing contrast. Each building is adorned with numerous balconies, adding to their architectural charm and providing residents with outdoor spaces. The buildings are situated approximately 10 meters from where we are standing, making them easily visible and a significant part of the immediate landscape.
