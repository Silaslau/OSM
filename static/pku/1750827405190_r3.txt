1. **Peking University:**
   Located from the center to about 10 degrees to the left, Peking University is immediately noticeable due to its large entrance gate adorned with a striking red banner. This prominent feature is situated approximately 5 meters from our current viewpoint. The gate serves as a welcoming landmark, and nearby, you can find Wūsì Road, which provides permissive access. Additionally, the Wumei store is an inclusive node in this area, adding to the bustling atmosphere.

2. **School of Journalism and Communication:**
   Positioned between 10 to 30 degrees to the right, the School of Journalism and Communication is a multi-story gray building characterized by its numerous windows. This structure stands about 30 meters away from us. The building's modern design and substantial height make it a significant feature in the scene. It is conveniently located adjacent to a road, ensuring easy access for students and visitors alike.
