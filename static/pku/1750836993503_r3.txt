1. Teaching Building No. 2:
Located from the left 70 to right 10 degree angle of our viewpoint, Teaching Building No. 2 is a prominent multi-story structure. The facade proudly displays the name 'Lee Shau Kee Building,' making it easily identifiable. This building is approximately 10 meters away from us, providing a close-up view of its architectural details. The building is surrounded by several adjoining features, including a road, a highway footway, and highway steps, which facilitate easy access. Additionally, within its vicinity, you can find the 3W咖啡餐吧（北京大学店） and Uncle M's CampusMart, adding a touch of convenience and leisure to the area.
