1. 24楼:
Located slightly to the left of the center of the scene, the 24楼 is a multi-story building that immediately catches the eye with its traditional architectural elements. The building features striking red windows that add a vibrant touch to its facade. The number "24" is prominently displayed, making it easy to identify. This building is approximately 5 meters away from our current viewpoint, giving us a clear and detailed look at its unique characteristics. Adjoining the 24楼 is a highway footway, providing a modern contrast to the traditional design of the building.
