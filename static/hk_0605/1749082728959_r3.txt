1. **T-Loft@Kai Tak:**
   Located between the left 60 to left 10 degree angle of our viewpoint, T-Loft@Kai Tak is a cluster of multiple tall residential buildings. These buildings are easily recognizable by their striking yellow and white facades, which add a vibrant touch to the skyline. The buildings are approximately 100 meters away from us. Surrounding T-Loft@Kai Tak, you can find several notable features including Tak Long Estate, Kai Fook Kui on Muk Long Street, and various other buildings, roads, and natural wood areas.

2. **Victoria Skye:**
   Positioned from the right 10 to right 70 degree angle, Victoria Skye is a prominent tall building that features a sophisticated mix of beige and dark colors. This building is much closer to us, at an approximate distance of just 10 meters. The area around Victoria Skye includes a variety of features such as a denotation avenue, a landuse flowerbed, private access points, barrier walls and fences, and a highway footway. These elements contribute to the dynamic and well-structured environment surrounding Victoria Skye.
