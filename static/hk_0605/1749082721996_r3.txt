1. Victoria Skye:
Located between the left 70 to left 30 degree angle of our viewpoint, Victoria Skye is a tall building that immediately catches the eye with its striking mix of blue and orange colors. The building features numerous balconies, adding to its dynamic appearance. It stands approximately 50 meters away from us. Surrounding Victoria Skye, you can see various adjoining features such as a denotation avenue, a landuse flowerbed, private access points, barrier walls, a barrier fence, and a highway footway.

2. Tower 1:
Positioned from the left 10 to right 50 degree angle, Tower 1 is another tall building characterized by its gray facade and multiple balconies. This building is much closer, at an estimated distance of 20 meters from our current location. Adjacent to Tower 1 is Tower 2, which is also an apartment building, creating a cohesive residential area.
