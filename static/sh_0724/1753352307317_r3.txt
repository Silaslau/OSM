1. JC Mandarin:
   Located from the left 30-degree to the right 10-degree angle of our viewpoint, the JC Mandarin is a striking glass building that prominently displays the brand name '郁滨文轩' on its facade. This modern structure, with its sleek and reflective glass exterior, stands approximately 20 meters away from us. Additionally, within this building, you will find the Consulate General of Ireland, making it not only a commercial hub but also a significant diplomatic site.
